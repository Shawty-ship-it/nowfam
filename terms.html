terms

the rules for using nowfam. short version: be kind, post only what you’re allowed to, and stay safe. last updated october 21, 2026.

using nowfam

  • you need to be 13 or older. use your real age and keep your password to yourself.
  • one person, one account. don’t pretend to be someone else.

be kind (we mean it)

  • no bullying, harassment, threats or hate.
  • no nudity or sexual content, no violence or gore, nothing illegal.
  • no spam, scams or fake accounts.
  • don’t post people who clearly don’t want to be posted, and respect it when friends ask you to take something down.
  • zero tolerance for anything that sexualizes or endangers minors. we remove it, ban the account and report it to the authorities.
  • there’s no place on nowfam for objectionable content or abusive users. we act on reports, usually within 24 hours.

your content

  • what you post is yours.
  • you give nowfam permission to store your moments and show them to the people you choose, only so the app works. that permission ends when you delete them (apart from short-lived backups or if the law requires us to keep something).
  • only post photos you have the right to share.

safety tools

  • you can report any moment, comment or person, and block anyone. blocking removes them from your friends and LinkUps, and they won’t be told.
  • we may remove content or suspend accounts that break these terms.

pings are just suggestions

  • nowfam never takes or posts a photo for you. a ping only suggests a moment; you decide.
  • stay aware of where you are. don’t use nowfam while driving, and don’t chase a pic somewhere unsafe.

the app as it is

  • nowfam is new and still changing. features may change or go away, and things may sometimes break.
  • nowfam is provided as-is, without promises that it will always work or be error-free, as far as the law allows. we’re not responsible for indirect losses, as far as the law allows.

ending things

  • you can delete your account anytime in settings.
  • we can suspend or delete accounts that break these terms or put others at risk.

changes

  • if these terms change, we’ll update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app for big changes. using nowfam after that means you’re okay with the new terms.
