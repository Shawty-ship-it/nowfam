privacy policy

what nowfam collects, who sees it, and how you stay in control. last updated october 21, 2026.

the short version

  • nowfam is for sharing moments with your friends, and only your friends. no public profiles, no strangers, no follower counts.
  • we don’t sell your data, we don’t show ads, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.
  • you choose what to share and with whom, and you can delete anything (or your whole account) whenever you want.

what we collect

  • your account: your email, your password (stored scrambled by our sign-in provider, we never see it), your username, display name, bio and profile picture (if you add one).
  • what you make: photos, captions, the vibe you pick or type, place names you type yourself, reactions, comments, LinkUps (the title, who you invited and who joined), friend requests, friends, blocks and reports.
  • invites: the invite codes you create and how many times each one was used.
  • that’s it. no contacts upload, no advertising IDs, no analytics trackers.

what stays on your phone

  • motion & fitness (steps, walking or running) and your approximate location are only used on your phone, while nowfam is open, to suggest a moment. they’re never sent to our servers or shown to friends.
  • your ping settings, ping history and quiet hours also stay on your phone.
  • weather pings: if you turn on location, nowfam asks Open-Meteo (a free weather service) about the weather in a rounded area of about 11 km (7 miles). nothing about you or your account is sent with it.
  • nowfam never attaches your real location to a photo. a place name only shows up if you type one.

who can see what

  • shared moments: only your friends. private memories: only you.
  • LinkUps: people in a LinkUp see who’s invited and who’s there. anyone who can see a photo tagged to a LinkUp (the poster’s friends) can tap it to see who said “i’m here”. people who were only invited, or said no, never show.
  • your username, display name, bio and profile picture can be seen by other signed-in nowfam users (for example when they search your username), so friends can find and add you. people you block can’t find you or see anything of yours.
  • reaction counts are only shown to you, on your own moments.

how we use it

  • to run nowfam: show your moments to your friends, deliver reactions, comments, invites and LinkUps, and keep your account working.
  • to keep people safe: we review reports and may remove content or accounts that break the rules.
  • we never sell your information, use it for ads, share it with data brokers, or use your photos to train AI.

services we use

  • Supabase: our database, private photo and profile picture storage, sign-in, and live updates.
  • Expo: builds the app and delivers app updates.
  • Open-Meteo: weather lookups (rounded area only, see above).
  • Apple: the App Store and, once we add them, push notifications. if you allow push notifications, we’ll store a device token so we can deliver them.
  • these services only process data to run nowfam for us.

how long we keep it

  • until you delete it or delete your account.
  • disappearing moments leave your friends’ feed after 24 hours but stay in your memories until you delete them.
  • deleted data may stay in our backups for a short time before it’s gone for good. reports may be kept as long as needed for safety.

your choices

  • turn location, motion, notifications and pings on or off anytime: settings → phone access, or your iPhone settings.
  • delete any moment, comment or reaction of yours.
  • delete your account: settings → delete account. this deletes your profile, moments, photos, comments, reactions, friends, LinkUps and invites.
  • you can ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it using the contact below.

age

  • nowfam is for people 13 and older. if we learn that someone under 13 made an account, we delete it.

security

  • photos are kept in private storage and only shown to people who are allowed to see them. our database checks who can see what on every request, and everything travels over encrypted connections.
  • no system is perfect, so use a password you don’t use anywhere else.

changes

  • if this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. for big changes, we’ll tell you in the app first.
