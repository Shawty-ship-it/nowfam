support

stuck, found a bug, or worried about something you saw? email us and a real person will reply.

email
nowfamteam@gmail.com
in the app: settings → contact & feedback

forgot your password

on the log in screen, type your email and tap forgot password?. open the link in the email on the same phone, then pick a new password.

report or block someone

tap ••• on a post to report it, or on someone’s profile to report or block them. reports are anonymous, and we act on them, usually within 24 hours. you can unblock people in settings → blocked people.

delete your account

settings → delete account. it deletes your profile, moments, photos, comments, reactions, friends, LinkUps and invites. you can also email us and we’ll do it for you.

your data

want a copy of your data, or something corrected? email us. see the privacy policy for what we keep and why.
